# List what is in the workspace

Connection: `https://api.pamba.app/mcp?readonly=true`

Prompt:

> What can you do with Pamba? Then list my avatars and the TikTok and Instagram accounts linked to each.

What happens: the model answers the first question from the product map the server sends when the client connects, then calls `list_avatars` and the account inventory tools. Nothing changes and no credits are spent.
